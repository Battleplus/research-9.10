## Reading of the table

The nearest-neighbor boundary is not “nobody combined these nouns.” The credible distinction is:

- A1: task-behavior labels remain the supervision; the world model supplies a cooperative, agent-time transition-effect signal. It must beat MAPT/attention and simple counterfactual credit baselines on held-out preference alignment, not merely improve return.
- A2: task-behavior labels remain the supervision; the world model supplies a coordination-relevant query score. It must beat policy-likelihood-only (PoLiCER), reward-model uncertainty/disagreement, and diversity/random selection at matched interaction and label budgets.
- RENEW’s imagined-rollout dynamics preference is a different supervision object. Any A paper that uses it must report it as a separate auxiliary signal, not as ordinary task preference.
