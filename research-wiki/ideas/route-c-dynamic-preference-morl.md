# Dynamic and Agent-Specific Preference Adaptation for Cooperative MARL

**stage:** `proposed`  ·  **outcome:** `pending`

Reserve direction: infer changing or agent-specific preferences under partial observability with adaptation-delay evaluation.

## Thesis
A genuine non-stationary preference stream, partially observed by the learner, may require online preference inference and adaptation rather than fixed preference conditioning.

## Key risks
Dynamic MORL, dynamic preference inference, network management, and agent-specific preference work already cover the broad formulation; without real changing preferences and adaptation metrics this is weak novelty.
